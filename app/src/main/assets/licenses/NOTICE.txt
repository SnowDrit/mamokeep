Optional OCR model packages
PP-OCRv5 mobile detection and East Slavic recognition models:
PaddlePaddle / PaddleOCR, Apache License 2.0.
Model cards, license, notices, source revisions and checksums accompany that package.
The original v1 package is downloaded separately after confirmation.
A fine-tuned V5 package is included in this APK and requires explicit activation.
It is also distributed as ocr-v5; v1 is retained for comparison and rollback.
The V5 recognizer is modified by MamoKeep. Its NOTICE, AUDIT and PROVENANCE record
historical preparation stages and are preserved byte-for-byte. Statements there
about a private/unpublished candidate describe that stage, before the 2026-10-25
publication. OCR remains early access; limited comparative testing is not
independent quality acceptance for arbitrary new receipts.
https://github.com/SnowDrit/MamoKeep-OCR-/releases/tag/ocr-v5
https://github.com/SnowDrit/MamoKeep-OCR-/releases/tag/ocr-v1
